A [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty entry: one line, at most 200
characters, roasting a smart fridge. The program writes the line to
`/output/entry.txt`.

## The line

> My smart fridge emailed my dentist a weekly report of my midnight
> snacks, with a chart titled 'Areas for Improvement.'

118 characters. The joke aims at the fridge's app and its habit of
reporting on you — a weekly snack report sent to the dentist, with a
chart titled "Areas for Improvement". No firmware-update gag, no
grocery-ordering gag, no spying-camera gag: the roast turns on the
fridge acting like a smug health coach with a mailing list.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`).
